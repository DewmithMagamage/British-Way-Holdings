BRITISH WAY HOLDINGS (PVT) LTD

Corporate Website Planning & Requirements Specification
Project Title
Development of the Official Website for British Way Holdings (Pvt) Ltd
 
1. Introduction
British Way Holdings (Pvt) Ltd is a diversified corporate organization operating in the fields of education, hospitality, and professional development. The organization manages several subsidiary companies and aims to establish a strong online corporate presence through a modern, professional, and responsive website.
The website will serve as the official digital platform for presenting the company’s profile, leadership, subsidiary companies, latest news, events, achievements, and corporate information.
 
2. Project Objectives
The primary objectives of the website are:
•	Establish a professional corporate identity online.
•	Showcase all companies operating under British Way Holdings.
•	Present company history, vision, mission, and values.
•	Introduce the corporate leadership team.
•	Publish news, announcements, and events.
•	Display achievements and milestones.
•	Provide contact information for stakeholders and visitors.
•	Enhance brand visibility and credibility.
